About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Mathematics at Peking University under the supervision of Professor Gang Tian. My research lies at the intersection of Kähler geometry, geometric analysis, and algebraic geometry, with particular interests in collapsing geometry, metric degeneration, and analysis on singular spaces.
